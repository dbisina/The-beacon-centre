The app

The Beacon Centre app gives you access to sermons, devotionals, live services, audio messages, Community Service Group (CSG) information, and giving. Most of the app works without an account. Creating an account (name, email, and a passcode) is optional and only needed for saving content, joining a CSG, or repeat giving.

Accounts

  • You're responsible for keeping your passcode private and for anything done under your account.
  • You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete account — see our Privacy Policy for what that removes.
  • We may suspend or remove an account used to abuse the app (e.g. spam, harassment, fraud).

Giving

  • Giving through the app is voluntary. Payments are processed by Paystack; we don't store your full card details.
  • Donations are generally non-refundable. If you believe a transaction was made in error, contact the church office through the app.

Community Service Groups

CSG information (meeting times, locations, updates) is provided by the relevant group's admin. Attendance and participation happen in person and are outside the app's control.

You may use them for personal, non-commercial purposes (e.g. sharing a link, listening for your own devotion). Please don't re-upload or redistribute our video/audio content elsewhere without permission.
