Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 August 2026

This policy explains what information The Beacon Centre mobile app collects, why, and how you can control or delete it. The app is built to work fully as a guest — creating an account is optional and only needed to save content across sessions, join a Community Service Group (CSG), or give.

Information we collect

What we collect depends on how you use the app.

  • If you create an account: your name, email address, and a 4–6 digit passcode. The passcode is stored as a one-way cryptographic hash — we cannot see or recover it.
  • Saved content, notes, and playback progress: which devotionals, sermons, or audio messages you've saved or noted, and how far you've gotten in a message, tied to your account so it's there next time you open the app.
  • Community Service Group (CSG) membership: which group you've joined and your RSVP status for group updates, if you choose to join one.
  • Giving records: if you give through the app, we store the transaction reference, amount, purpose, and status. Card payments are processed by Paystack — we never see or store your full card number. We may store a tokenized authorization code from Paystack so you can give again without re-entering your card.
  • Prayer requests and contact messages: whatever you choose to submit (name, email, phone if given, and your message), sent to our pastoral team or church office to respond to.
  • Push notification token: a device identifier used only to deliver notifications you've opted into (e.g. live service alerts), if you enable them.
  • Anonymous usage data: for guests and account holders alike, we log which content is viewed or played (e.g. "this sermon was watched for 4 minutes") tied to an anonymous device identifier, not your name or email, to understand what's useful.

How we use this information

  • To run the core features you use: saving content, resuming playback, CSG updates, giving, prayer requests.
  • To send notifications you've explicitly opted into.
  • To respond to prayer requests and messages sent to the church office.
  • To keep basic records of giving as required for financial accountability.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Third-party services we use

  • Paystack — processes card payments for giving. See Paystack's own privacy policy.
  • Cloudinary — hosts images, audio, and video thumbnails used in the app.
  • YouTube / Google — sermon videos are streamed via YouTube's player.
  • Google Gemini — generates devotional card background images from the day's verse text only; no personal or account data is sent to it.

Your choices

  • Delete your account: open the app, go to Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your account, saved content, notes, playback progress, and CSG membership. It cannot be undone.
  • Giving transaction records are kept even after account deletion, disassociated from your account, since financial recordkeeping requires it — they're no longer linked to your name or email in the app.
  • You can sign out at any time without deleting your account; your data stays as-is for next time.
  • You can use nearly all of the app — sermons, devotionals, live service, audio — without ever creating an account.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we'll update the date above and, where appropriate, notify users in the app.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent through the app's Settings → Contact the developers or Contact the church office.
